About

A practitioner first, a company second.

I'm Omar — I've spent the last 14+ years inside regulated institutions that cannot afford a wrong number: banks, asset managers, and insurers where a broken variance report isn't an inconvenience, it's a control failure. My focus sits at the intersection of Microsoft Fabric and financial services domain fluency — semantic modelling, DAX, and governed lakehouse architecture built specifically for FP&A, treasury and credit risk reporting.

Milestone BI is the practice I run, but the work is mine — every model, every measure, every governance decision is something I've built and stood behind personally. Consulting engagements double as design partnerships: real problems from real reporting teams inform the tools I build next, including an AI-assisted financial commentary engine I'm currently developing to automate variance narratives at month-end close.
